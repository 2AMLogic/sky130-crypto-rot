- **Claim**: `rtl/crypto_rot_top.v` -- the top level integrating `rtl/aes/aes_core.v`, `rtl/hmac/hmac_sha256.v`, `rtl/keyladder/keyladder.v` and `rtl/drbg/drbg_top.v` (which embeds `trng_if`) behind the ratified register bus (0005) and register map (0009) -- runs the published AES and HMAC known-answer vectors end to end through the bus with slot keys (FIPS 197 both directions with the vector key loaded into a slot; RFC 4231 cases 1-3 with the case key loaded; case 4's 131-byte key is not bus-reachable in v1 and case 5 is truncated output, both out of scope by 0009), derives slot keys that match `verification/model/kdf.py` and produces DRBG output that matches `verification/model/ctr_drbg.py` seeded from the sibling stub's words; refuses every access class 0009 makes a violation (undefined address, write to a read-only register, write while the addressed engine is busy, read of a write-only location) with `err_o`, a zero read value, the `FAULT_CLS` bit and the ERROR pending bit; escalates (zeroize + `FAULT_ZEROIZED`) on a second violation within one operation and on any violation during key load; and performs every `target-spec.md` section 5 zeroize event (software `ZEROIZE`, synchronized TRNG alarm through `ALARM_ZEROIZED` and its recovery at the source, TRNG `gated` to `RESEED_REQUIRED` with keys kept, reset, and zeroize during an in-flight AES operation). A correctness claim for this block's simulated behaviour only; it is not a key-isolation proof (the FORMAL sibling is #46's second child) and makes no side-channel, timing, entropy or certification claim (0008).
