# Record 20261114-080202-59bc1ff

- **Record ID**: 20261114-080202-59bc1ff
- **Claim**: `rtl/trng_if/trng_if.v` implements this block's side of `spec/trng-interface.md` sections 3.1-3.4 (2-flop synchronizer on the 50 kHz sample clock with `t_rise`/`t_fall` ticks; coherent sampling of `out_data`/`out_valid`/`gated`/`startup_done`/`alarm` at `t_fall`; `out_ready` held exactly one sample period so exactly one word moves; a separate 2-flop `alarm` path to zeroize; a write-only bus sequencer with the three allowed transactions and `bus_re` tied 0), and `drbg_top` zeroizes the DRBG `Key`/`V`, working registers and seed buffer on a TRNG alarm. A simulation claim at the stated operating points; it says nothing about the sibling's entropy (provisional until silicon), about clock-domain timing (no STA was run: the half-period constraint is listed in `rtl/trng_if/README.md`), or about any property beyond the cases below.
- **Design provenance**: RTL, pre-synthesis simulation (Icarus). The sibling is modelled by a port-protocol stub (`trngmodel.py`: outputs change only just after a rising edge of the sample clock; pop when `out_ready && out_valid` at a rising edge; ALARM sticky until a W1C write; it does not mask `out_valid` when gated) and, in the two benches below, by the sibling's own RTL at the `reuse.lock.json` pin -- once with its start-up/conditioner/RCT parameters shortened and once with no parameter overrides at all.
- **Run configuration**: `./scripts/setup-env.sh` then `./verification/rtl/trng_if/run.sh --record`. Requests are generated by `mk_req.py`; engine icarus, `random_seed` 1. Sample-clock periods are not a multiple of the core period (ratio*20 ns + 3 ns, 7 ns phase) so the two clocks drift against each other.
- **Benches and results**:
  - `trng_if` alone vs the stub, 40:1 ratio (2/2 tests passed, includes the 1000:1 collection case): bring-up writes (`CTRL = 0b011` once, nothing else), 16-word seed and 8-word reseed with exactly one pop per word and the seed equal to the popped words in arrival order, seed held with no further pops while ready and cleared on release, `gated` and `startup_done = 0` mid-seed (partial seed discarded, collection restarts from word 0), a one-period `gated` pulse, no word accepted when `gated` was high at the previous or current sample (checked by the stub from its own history), alarm: seed buffer zero within the bound and held zero with a request pending, `ALARM = 0b111` written only after zeroize completed, then `SOFT_RST` and re-enable, `alarm_zeroized` low again, a new seed collected, software zeroize. `SUMMARY trng_if ratio=1000:1 stub (collection only) pops=24 writes=1 first_failure=None failures=0 ; SUMMARY trng_if ratio=40:1 stub pops=107 writes=4 first_failure=None failures=0`
